# Supplier and affiliate research (roadmap 4.14)

Desk research into UK retailers that sell panels, charge controllers and inverters to the public, and the affiliate programmes they run. It feeds the applications in roadmap task 4.1.

**Checked:** 2026-09-30, from public web pages only. Nobody has applied or contacted a retailer yet. Commission rates and cookie lengths change, and some figures below come from third-party directories rather than the retailer, so confirm the terms in the network dashboard before relying on them. Each figure is tagged with its source type:

- **(R)**: the retailer's or brand's own page, or its network profile (Awin, Paid On Results).
- **(3P)**: a third-party affiliate directory. Treat it as indicative only.

## Findings by retailer

### Solar specialists (consumer-facing)

**Bimble Solar** (bimblesolar.com)
- Sells: off-grid and leisure kits, panels, Victron, batteries, and some grid-tied gear. Consumer-facing. 14 catalogue links already.
- Programme: in-house (OpenCart affiliate module), free to join. **2% standard commission** (R). Cookie length and payment terms are in its affiliate T&Cs, which weren't reachable without an account.
- Notes: low rate, but the best overlap with the off-grid half of the catalogue. Ask whether the rate rises with volume.
- Source: [bimblesolar.com affiliate login page](https://www.bimblesolar.com/index.php?route=affiliate%2Flogin)

**Voltacon Solar** (voltaconsolar.com)
- Sells: its own-brand panels and controllers (Voltacon is also a catalogue manufacturer), plus other brands. Consumer-facing. 24 catalogue links, the most of any retailer.
- Programme: on **Paid On Results**, **1% commission, 60-day cookie**, auto-accept (R). A 2021 Voltacon blog post advertised **3%** through an in-house signup (R, but dated). Ask which one is current.
- Restrictions: no PPC bidding on "VoltaconSolar", its domain or misspellings (R).
- Sources: [Paid On Results profile](https://www.paidonresults.com/merchants/voltaconsolar.html), [Voltacon blog, 2021](https://blog.voltaconsolar.com/2021/06/01/join-our-affiliate-program-and-earn-3-of-order-value/)

**Sunstore Solar** (sunstore.co.uk)
- Sells: 12 V kits, panels, inverters, storage and Victron leisure kits; in business since 2011. No catalogue links yet.
- Programme: in-house. **5% on all qualifying sales, 30-day cookie**, paid monthly, free to join (R).
- Source: [Sunstore affiliate FAQ](https://www.sunstore.co.uk/faq/affiliate-program/)

**Sunshine Solar** (sunshinesolar.co.uk)
- Sells: small panels, solar chargers and related off-grid products. No catalogue links yet; check how much of the catalogue it actually stocks.
- Programme: in-house, apply by contact form. **6% starting commission**, higher for active affiliates (R). Cookie length and payment terms are not published.
- Source: [Sunshine Solar affiliate page](https://www.sunshinesolar.co.uk/affiliate)

**TradeSparky** (tradesparky.com)
- Sells: electrical wholesale including panels and hybrid inverters, to "trade professionals and the general public" (R, homepage). 21 catalogue links, the second-largest source.
- Programme: **none found** on the site or on the main networks. Worth a direct email, because it is our biggest consumer-accessible grid-tied source.

**Midsummer** (midsummerenergy.co.uk / midsummerwholesale.co.uk)
- Now primarily a trade wholesaler with trade accounts for installers and resellers; it still sells some off-grid kits for boats and motorhomes. **No programme found.**

**Fogstar** (fogstar.co.uk)
- Sells: own-brand batteries and Fogstar-badged LuxpowerTek inverters directly to consumers (5 inverter records in the catalogue).
- Programme: **none found**. The only scheme found is a customer referral bonus tied to a partner energy service, which isn't an affiliate programme. Worth a direct email; its inverter plus battery bundles are high-ticket.

**Segen, CCL Components, Waxman Energy**: trade distributors with no affiliate schemes found. Keep them as price references only.

### Leisure and Victron dealers

Victron doesn't sell directly; it sells through dealers. The catalogue has 36 Victron records, so a Victron dealer with a programme matters.

**Butler Technik** (butlertechnik.com)
- Sells: authorised Victron dealer (MPPT controllers, chargers, inverters, panels, kits) plus Webasto/Eberspächer heaters. Since 1998.
- Programme: on **Awin**. **2–5% commission, 30-day cookie**, approval within 45 days; banners and **live product feeds** offered (R).
- Restrictions: no Google Ads without permission and a keyword disclosure; no unsolicited email (R).
- Sources: [Butler Technik affiliate page](https://www.butlertechnik.com/affiliate-program), [Awin profile](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/31291)

**Other Victron leisure shops not yet checked for programmes**: 12 Volt Planet, Leisureshopdirect, Van Junkies, Wired Campers and Jacksons Leisure. 12 Volt Planet is a long-standing Victron specialist and worth an email.

### General retailers and marketplaces

**City Plumbing** (cityplumbing.co.uk)
- Sells: consumer-facing via its electrical division, including panels, inverters, batteries and plug-in solar kits; 370 branches with click and collect. 8 catalogue links. Listed as one of the two main UK consumer retailers of plug-in solar kits.
- Programme: on **Rakuten Advertising** (since 2024) and **Partnerize** (since 2025); also listed on FlexOffers. About **1.5% commission, up to 30-day cookie** (3P).
- Sources: [Affilitizer](https://www.affilitizer.com/programs/cityplumbing.co.uk), [LinkClicky](https://linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/city-plumbing/), [Plug-in solar providers directory](https://www.pluginsolarexplained.co.uk/providers/)

**Amazon UK (Associates)**
- Stocks Renogy, EPEver and some Victron and leisure items (third-party sellers vary). Not a good source for grid-tied gear.
- Commission: **5%** for Home Improvement, Automotive, Power & Hand Tools; **3%** for "All Other Categories"; **2.5%** for Appliances and Mobile Electronics (R). Which category a given charge controller falls in has to be checked per product.
- Restrictions that affect the app: Amazon prices may only be shown if they come from its product API and are fresh (roadmap 6.4); a 24-hour cookie; the Amazon disclosure wording (6.2) must be added.
- Source: [Associates fee schedule](https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ)

**eBay Partner Network**: 1–4% depending on category, electronics at the low end (3P). Low priority.

**Screwfix**: up to 1%, 14-day cookie, and **closed to new applicants** (R, [Screwfix affiliates](https://www.screwfix.com/help/affiliates)).

**Skimlinks**: not a retailer, but an aggregator that auto-affiliates links to about 48,500 merchants and keeps 25% of the commission (3P). It could cover small retailers that join a network we don't, without separate applications. Its inclusion of any given UK solar shop would need checking.

### Brand-direct stores

**Renogy UK** (uk.renogy.com)
- Catalogue fit: 4 panels and 5 controllers, and the leisure and off-grid audience it targets.
- Programme: on **Awin**, **30-day cookie** (R); commission is reported as about **6% average** (3P). Last-click attribution, de-duplicated against Renogy's own channels.
- Restrictions: no paid search on the Renogy brand, typos or close variants; only discount codes distributed through Awin earn commission (R).
- Sources: [Awin profile](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/127461), [Renogy UK affiliate page](https://uk.renogy.com/pages/affiliate-program)

**EcoFlow UK**
- Programme: on **Awin** (and CJ). **5% minimum, 7-day cookie**, average order about £1,000, approval in 2–3 business days (R).
- Restrictions: bans hidden links, pop-up extensions, impersonation and "search engine placement"; reverses commission on refunds (R).
- Catalogue fit: none today. It becomes relevant with portable power stations and plug-in solar (roadmap 3.10, 7.12).
- Sources: [EcoFlow UK affiliate page](https://uk.ecoflow.com/pages/affiliate-program), [Awin profile](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/51797)

**Anker SOLIX UK**: Anker's UK programme covers SOLIX. **8% commission, 30-day cookie** (3P, quoting Anker); also on Webgains at 8–15% (3P). Catalogue fit: none until 3.10/7.12. Sources: [Anker UK affiliates](https://www.anker.com/uk/become-an-affiliate), [Webgains](https://www.webgains.com/front/publisher/program/view/programID/310160)

**Jackery UK**: on **Awin**, **6% base, 30-day cookie**, no trademark bidding (R). Catalogue fit: none until 3.10. Source: [Awin profile](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/30413)

**Bluetti UK**: in-house. **5% standard, up to 10%, 30-day cookie, £100 minimum payout** (3P). Catalogue fit: none until 3.10. Source: [Bluetti UK affiliate page](https://bluettipower.co.uk/pages/affiliate-program-1)

**Victron**: no direct sales, so no brand programme; go through dealers (Butler Technik, Bimble, Sunstore).

## What this means for the app

1. **Off-grid and leisure is well served.** Renogy, Bimble, Sunstore, Butler Technik and Voltacon all have programmes and between them stock most of the Victron, Renogy, EPEver and Voltacon catalogue.
2. **Grid-tied and hybrid is poorly served.** Most panels (AIKO, JA, LONGi, Jinko, Trina) and hybrid inverters (GivEnergy, Sunsynk, Solis, Fox, SolaX, Growatt) are bought through trade distributors or TradeSparky, none of which has a programme. City Plumbing (≈1.5%) and Voltacon (1–3%) are the only affiliate-able consumer routes found. For that segment, the installer-quote lead form (roadmap 4.13) probably earns more than product links.
3. **Rates are low for the specialists** (1–6%) and higher for portable-power brands (5–8%) with average orders around £1,000. That supports doing 3.10 (portable power stations) and 7.12 (plug-in solar) early.
4. **Restrictions to design for (4.3–4.5):**
   - Brand-keyword PPC bans (Renogy, Voltacon, Jackery; Butler Technik needs permission). Irrelevant unless we run ads, but record them.
   - Renogy only credits Awin-distributed voucher codes, so don't show codes from elsewhere.
   - Amazon price display rules (6.4) and its 24-hour cookie.
   - Awin and Paid On Results both support deep links, so the rewrite-rule approach in 4.5 works for most of the list. The in-house schemes (Bimble, Sunstore, Sunshine) each use their own link format.
   - Butler Technik offers product feeds, which could feed prices directly (4.6) instead of scraping.
5. **Network sign-ups needed:** Awin (Renogy, Butler Technik, and later EcoFlow and Jackery), Paid On Results (Voltacon), Rakuten or Partnerize (City Plumbing), plus three in-house schemes. Awin charges publishers nothing but may ask for a small refundable deposit at sign-up; check when applying.

## Ranked list: apply in this order

Ranked by catalogue overlap × commission × ease of joining.

1. **Renogy UK** (Awin, ≈6%, 30 days): brand-direct for 9 catalogue products, and it opens the Awin account the others need.
2. **Bimble Solar** (in-house, 2%): largest affiliate-able overlap with the off-grid catalogue, 14 links already.
3. **Voltacon Solar** (Paid On Results, 1–3%, 60 days, auto-accept): our most-linked retailer and brand.
4. **Sunstore Solar** (in-house, 5%, 30 days): off-grid and Victron kits at a good rate.
5. **Butler Technik** (Awin, 2–5%, 30 days, product feed): covers the 36 Victron records.
6. **City Plumbing** (Rakuten/Partnerize, ≈1.5%): the only mainstream consumer route for grid-tied and plug-in gear.
7. **Sunshine Solar** (in-house, 6%): check stock overlap first.
8. **Contact directly** (no programme found): TradeSparky, Fogstar, 12 Volt Planet.
9. **Later, with 3.10 / 7.12:** EcoFlow, Anker SOLIX, Jackery, Bluetti.
10. **Defer:** Amazon Associates (only once 6.4 is decided), eBay Partner Network, Skimlinks as a fallback.

### Scanner limit
`searchDistributorLink` (`verification_scripts/lib/serper.js`) puts every whitelisted domain into **one** query as OR'd `site:` filters. After these additions the lists hold 17 (panels) and 22 (controllers) domains. Google caps queries at about 32 words and returns about 10 organic results, so much further growth risks truncated queries and crowded-out retailers. Before adding more, change the scanner to split the whitelist into batches of about 10 domains per query (roughly doubling Serper usage).
